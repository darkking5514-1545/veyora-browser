2. Information We Collect

Veyora Browser is designed with privacy in mind. Below is a clear breakdown of what is and is not collected:

Data Type Collected? Details
Browsing history, bookmarks, downloads list Stored locally only This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or any server we control.
Personally identifiable information (name, address, phone number, email) Not collected We do not require account creation and do not collect personal identifiers.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate core browser functionality (browsing, bookmarks, downloads, incognito mode, ad blocking, extensions).
  • To deliver rewarded video ads that let you unlock additional ad-blocking quota, via Google AdMob.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve app stability using anonymous crash/diagnostic signals, where available.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Veyora Browser integrates the following categories of third-party services, each of which operates under its own privacy policy and may independently collect data as described therein:

  • Mobile Advertising Network — used to display rewarded video ads. The advertising SDK may collect the Advertising ID and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Mobile Platform Services — provides core Android platform functionality and may collect basic diagnostic data as governed by the platform provider's own privacy policy.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party providers to understand how they handle the data collected through their services.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your browsing history, bookmarks, saved downloads, and extension settings are stored locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. We do not operate servers that store this data, and we do not have access to it. We recommend using your device's built-in security features (screen lock, encryption) to protect locally stored data. While we take reasonable care in how the App is built, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Permissions Used

  • Internet Access — required for core browsing functionality and to load web pages.
  • Storage — required to save downloaded files and manage bookmarks/browsing data on your device.
  • Notifications — used to alert you about download completions and relevant app updates.

Veyora Browser does not request permissions unrelated to its stated browser functionality.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & Deletion of Local Data: Since browsing history, bookmarks, and downloads are stored locally, you can view, manage, or delete this data at any time directly within the App's settings, or by clearing app data/uninstalling the App via your device settings.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising by enabling the "Opt out of Ads Personalization" option in your Android device's privacy/ads settings.
  • Resetting Advertising ID: You may reset your Advertising ID at any time from your device settings.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored data (history, bookmarks, downloads) is retained on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. Any data collected by third-party services (such as the advertising network) is retained according to those providers' own retention policies, which are outside of our control.
